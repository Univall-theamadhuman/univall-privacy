Child Safety Standards

Feel Everything.

Last updated 11 September 2026

Univall is committed to protecting minors from exploitation, abuse, and harmful content. We take a zero-tolerance stance on Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) material and behavior. This page describes the standards and mechanisms we apply.

Our Commitment

  • Univall prohibits any content that sexualizes, endangers, or exploits minors.
  • We prohibit grooming behavior, solicitation of minors, and any attempt to establish inappropriate contact with a minor.
  • Accounts found violating these standards are permanently removed, and where required, reported to the relevant authorities and organizations such as NCMEC.

In-App Reporting

Every user profile, post, and chat in Univall has a built-in Report option. Reports can be filed under these categories:

  • Unusual Activity
  • Uncomfortable Interaction
  • Profile Authenticity Concern
  • Content Concern
  • Other

Reports are reviewed and acted on, including account suspension or removal where our standards have been violated.

Prevention Measures

  • Mandatory email verification before an account can access core features
  • Firebase App Check and Play Integrity API to block fake, automated, or modified-app access
  • Security rules restricting data access strictly to verified users
