Child Safety Standards - Univall

Last updated: July 27, 2026

Univall is committed to protecting children and preventing child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) on our platform. This page outlines our standards and practices.

1. Zero Tolerance Policy

Univall has a zero-tolerance policy toward any content that sexualizes, exploits, or endangers minors. Any account found to be engaging in such behavior will be permanently banned and, where applicable, reported to the relevant authorities and organizations such as the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC).

2. Age Requirement

Univall is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly allow children under 13 to create accounts on our platform.

3. Reporting Mechanism

Users who encounter content or behavior that raises child safety concerns can report it by contacting us directly through the contact information below. We review all reports promptly and take appropriate action, including account suspension, content removal, and reporting to law enforcement where required.

4. Moderation and Enforcement

We actively monitor our platform for violations of this policy and take swift action against accounts or content that violate these standards, including immediate removal of content and permanent suspension of offending accounts.

6. Compliance

Univall complies with all applicable child safety laws and regulations, and cooperates with regional and national authorities in matters relating to child safety.
