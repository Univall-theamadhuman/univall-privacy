Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 September 2026

Univall ("we", "our", "us") operates the Univall mobile application (the "App"), a social platform that lets people share posts, stories, chat, make voice/video calls, and post short videos through V Share. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

By creating an account or using Univall, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1Information We Collect

Account information

  • Name, email address, and phone number (if provided) at sign-up
  • Profile details you choose to add — profile photo, bio, and similar
  • Authentication data if you sign in with Google

Content you create

  • Posts, stories, comments, and V Share videos
  • Chat messages and media (photos, videos, files) shared in conversations
  • Voice and video call sessions made through the app's calling feature

Automatically collected information

  • Device information and app interaction data, used to keep the app stable and secure
  • Approximate presence/activity status (e.g. "last seen", "online") for chat features

2How We Use Your Information

  • To operate core features — feed, stories, chat, notifications, and V Share
  • To connect voice and video calls between users
  • To maintain your account, including login, verification, and account recovery
  • To detect and prevent spam, fake accounts, and abuse
  • To improve app performance and fix bugs

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3How Information Is Shared

We share limited data only where necessary to provide the service:

These providers only process data on our behalf and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes.

4Your Choices & Controls

  • Account deletion: Settings → Danger Zone → Delete Account. This permanently removes your profile and associated data.
  • Reporting: You can report a user directly from their profile or a chat (Unusual Activity, Uncomfortable Interaction, Profile Authenticity, Content Concern, or Other).
  • Blocking & muting: Available from any chat or profile screen.

5Data Security

We use Firebase security rules, Firebase App Check, and Play Integrity API to help ensure that only genuine, verified requests from the real Univall app can access your data. Email verification is required before an account can fully access the platform.

6Children's Privacy

Univall is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the details below so we can remove it. See also our Child Safety Standards page.

7Data Retention

We retain your account and content for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your data is removed from our active systems, except where retention is required for legal or security purposes.

8Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as Univall grows and adds new features. We will update the "Last updated" date above whenever changes are made. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
