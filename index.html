Privacy Policy for Univall

Last updated: July 23, 2026

Univall ("we", "our", "us") operates the Univall mobile application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App.

1. Information We Collect

To provide and improve our service, we collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Name, username, email address, phone number, and profile photo when you register.
  • Content You Share: Photos, videos, captions, stories, comments, and messages you post or send within the App.
  • Chat & Communication Data: Messages you send and receive through the App's chat feature.
  • Usage Data: Information on how you interact with the App, such as posts liked, saved, or viewed.
  • Device Information: Basic device data such as device type and operating system, used for app functionality and troubleshooting.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the App
  • Enable communication between users (chat, comments, notifications)
  • Personalize your experience and content feed
  • Improve and develop new features
  • Ensure the security and integrity of our platform

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. Information such as your profile, posts, and stories may be visible to other users of the App as part of its normal social functionality. We may share data with trusted third-party service providers (such as cloud storage and media hosting providers) solely to operate the App.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using industry-standard cloud infrastructure (Firebase/Google Cloud). We implement reasonable technical measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

5. Your Choices

  • You can edit or delete your profile information at any time within the App.
  • You can delete posts, stories, and messages you have created.
  • You can block or report other users.

6. Children's Privacy

Univall is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
